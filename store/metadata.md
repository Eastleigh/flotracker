## Full Description

Bloom is the period and cycle tracker that respects your time and your privacy.

No 55-step sign-up flows. No accounts. No data leaving your device. Just open the app, tell it your cycle basics, and you're ready in 30 seconds.

**What Bloom does:**

🌸 **Cycle Dashboard** — See your current cycle day, phase, and a beautiful ring visualization at a glance.

📅 **Smart Calendar** — Color-coded monthly view shows your period days, fertile window, and ovulation day. Tap any day to see logged details.

📝 **Full Symptom Logging** — Track flow intensity, 10 physical symptoms, 8 moods, intimacy, and free-text notes. Takes seconds, not minutes.

📊 **Insights & Analytics** — See your average cycle length, top symptoms and moods over time, cycle regularity score, and educational phase breakdowns.

🔮 **Cycle Predictions** — Smart algorithm learns from your history (last 3-6 cycles) to predict your next period, fertile window, and ovulation date.

🔒 **100% Private** — All your data stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no third-party tracking. Your body, your data, your business.

**Why Bloom is different:**

• **30-second setup** — 3 simple steps vs. competitors' 50+ screen onboarding
• **No paywall nags** — Full features, free. No premium tier, no trial expirations
• **No account required** — No email, no phone number, no social login
• **Clean design** — Warm, calming interface that doesn't overwhelm
• **Actually private** — We can't sell your data because we never have it

**Understanding Your Cycle:**

Bloom helps you understand the four phases of your menstrual cycle:
• 🌺 Menstrual (Days 1-5) — Your period
• 🌱 Follicular (Days 6-13) — Energy rises
• 🌸 Ovulation (Day ~14) — Peak fertility
• 🍂 Luteal (Days 15-28) — PMS may appear

Each phase comes with tips to help you feel your best.

Download Bloom and take control of your cycle — simply and privately.
